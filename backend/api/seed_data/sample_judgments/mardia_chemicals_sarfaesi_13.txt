MARDIA CHEMICALS LTD. V. UNION OF INDIA & ORS. (2004) 4 SCC 311 SUPREME COURT OF INDIA
Bench: 3-Judge Bench (B.N. Kirpal CJI, N. Santosh Hegde, B.P. Singh JJ.)

STATUTORY RATIO DECIDENDI & SARFAESI ACT SECTION 13(2) NOTICE RULES:
1. Section 13(2) of the SARFAESI Act, 2002 empowers secured creditors (banks and financial institutions) to issue a statutory 60-day demand notice to borrowers whose accounts have been classified as Non-Performing Assets (NPA).
2. The Supreme Court upheld the constitutional validity of SARFAESI Act while mandating that secured creditors must consider representations or objections filed by the borrower under Section 13(3A) and communicate reasoned decisions prior to taking physical possession of secured assets under Section 13(4).
3. Litigants aggrieved by Section 13(4) possession notices must approach the Debt Recovery Tribunal (DRT) under Section 17 rather than filing premature writ petitions under Article 226.
