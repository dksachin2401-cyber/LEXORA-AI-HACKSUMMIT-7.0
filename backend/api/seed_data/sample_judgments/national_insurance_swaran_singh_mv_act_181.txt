NATIONAL INSURANCE CO. LTD. V. SWARAN SINGH & ORS. (2004) 3 SCC 297 SUPREME COURT OF INDIA
Bench: 3-Judge Bench (V.N. Khare CJI, S.B. Sinha, A.R. Lakshmanan JJ.)

STATUTORY RATIO DECIDENDI & MOTOR VEHICLES ACT SECTION 181 FINDINGS:
1. Section 181 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 imposes strict statutory penalties and compounding fines for driving a motor vehicle without a valid driving license or in violation of statutory license terms under Section 3 and 5.
2. The Supreme Court held that mere absence or fake driving license of the driver at the time of accident does not automatically discharge the insurance company of its liability toward third-party claims unless the insurer proves intentional and willful breach by the vehicle owner.
3. Where a driver drives without a valid license under Section 181 MV Act, the police authorities are empowered under Section 207 to seize the vehicle and levy compulsory compounding fines.
