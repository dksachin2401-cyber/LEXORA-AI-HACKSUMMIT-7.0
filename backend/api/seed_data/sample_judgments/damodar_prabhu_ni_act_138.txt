DAMODAR S. PRABHU V. SAYED BABALAL H. (2010) 5 SCC 663 SUPREME COURT OF INDIA
Bench: 3-Judge Bench (K.G. Balakrishnan CJI, P. Sathasivam, J.M. Panchal JJ.)

STATUTORY RATIO DECIDENDI & NEGOTIABLE INSTRUMENTS ACT SECTION 138 GUIDELINES:
1. Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881 creates criminal liability for the dishonor of a cheque drawn for the discharge of any debt or other liability.
2. Mandatory statutory requirement: The payee or holder in due course must issue a formal legal demand notice in writing to the drawer within 30 days of receiving information from the bank regarding cheque dishonor.
3. The drawer must be granted a statutory period of 15 days from the date of receipt of notice to make payment before a criminal complaint under Section 138 can be legally instituted.
4. Supreme Court issued comprehensive national guidelines encouraging early compounding of cheque bounce offenses under Section 147 NI Act to prevent clogging of judicial dockets.
