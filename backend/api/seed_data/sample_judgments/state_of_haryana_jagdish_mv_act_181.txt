STATE OF HARYANA V. JAGDISH (2010) 4 SCC 216 SUPREME COURT OF INDIA
Bench: 2-Judge Bench

STATUTORY RATIO DECIDENDI & MOTOR VEHICLES ACT REGULATORY COMPLIANCE:
1. Driving a commercial or private motor vehicle without possessing an active, valid driving license issued by a competent Licensing Authority is a statutory offense under Section 181 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988.
2. The Supreme Court mandated strict regulatory enforcement across state highways, directing transport authorities to register compounding challans and enforce statutory license verification via digital e-Challan repositories.
3. Compounding fines under Section 181 MV Act must be deposited with the regional transport office prior to release of impounded vehicles under Section 207.
