GURBAKSH SINGH SIBBIA & ORS. V. STATE OF PUNJAB (1980) 2 SCC 565 SUPREME COURT OF INDIA
Bench: 5-Judge Constitution Bench (Y.V. Chandrachud CJI, P.N. Bhagwati, V.R. Krishna Iyer, R.S. Sarkaria, A.D. Koshal JJ.)

STATUTORY RATIO DECIDENDI & CODE OF CRIMINAL PROCEDURE SECTION 438 ANTICIPATORY BAIL:
1. Section 438 of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973 confers extra-ordinary judicial discretion on the High Court and Court of Session to grant anticipatory bail to any person apprehending arrest on an accusation of having committed a non-bailable offense.
2. The Supreme Court held that personal liberty guaranteed under Article 21 of the Constitution mandates a liberal and purposive construction of Section 438 CrPC.
3. Anticipatory bail order should not be restricted by blanket duration limits unless specific statutory conditions or gravity of offense warrants judicial restriction.
