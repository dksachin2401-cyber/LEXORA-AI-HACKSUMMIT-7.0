JUSTICE K.S. PUTTASWAMY (RETD.) V. UNION OF INDIA (2017) 10 SCC 1
SUPREME COURT OF INDIA
Case No: Writ Petition (Civil) No. 494 of 2012
Bench: 9-Judge Constitution Bench

SUMMARY & RATIO DECIDENDI:
Unanimously affirmed that the Right to Privacy is a fundamental right protected under Article 21 and Part III of the Constitution of India.

KEY PRINCIPLES:
1. Fundamental Right to Privacy: Privacy is intrinsic to life, dignity, and personal autonomy.
2. Proportionality Test: State interference with privacy must satisfy legality, legitimate state objective, and proportionality.
3. Information Privacy: Protection of personal data and digital identity against surveillance.

STATUTORY SECTIONS REFERENCED:
- Article 21, Article 19, Article 14 of the Constitution of India
- Aadhaar (Targeted Delivery of Financial and Other Subsidies, Benefits and Services) Act, 2016
