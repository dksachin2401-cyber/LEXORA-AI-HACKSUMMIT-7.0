CONSUMER PROTECTION ACT, 2019 — ACT NO. 35 OF 2019
Source: Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution / Legislative Department / India Code
Authority Level: 1 (Primary Consumer Rights Statute)
Jurisdiction: Republic of India
Status: IN_FORCE

CHAPTER II — CONSUMER PROTECTION COUNCILS

Section 2: Definitions.
(7) "consumer" means any person who buys any goods for a consideration which has been paid or promised or partly paid and partly promised, or under any system of deferred payment and includes any user of such goods other than the person who buys such goods for consideration paid or promised or partly paid or partly promised, when such use is made with the approval of such person, but does not include a person who obtains such goods for resale or for any commercial purpose.

CHAPTER IV — CONSUMER DISPUTES REDRESSAL COMMISSION

Section 34: Jurisdiction of District Commission.
(1) Subject to the other provisions of this Act, the District Commission shall have jurisdiction to entertain complaints where the value of the goods or services paid as consideration does not exceed fifty lakh rupees.
(2) A complaint shall be instituted in a District Commission within the local limits of whose jurisdiction the complainant resides or personally works for gain, or the cause of action, wholly or in part, arises.
