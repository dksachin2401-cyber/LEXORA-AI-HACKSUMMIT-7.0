CONSTITUTION OF INDIA — AUTHORITATIVE LEGISLATIVE TEXT
Source: Legislative Department, Ministry of Law and Justice, Government of India / India Code
Authority Level: 1 (Supreme Constitutional Text)
Jurisdiction: Republic of India
Status: IN_FORCE

PREAMBLE:
WE, THE PEOPLE OF INDIA, having solemnly resolved to constitute India into a SOVEREIGN SOCIALIST SECULAR DEMOCRATIC REPUBLIC and to secure to all its citizens: JUSTICE, social, economic and political; LIBERTY of thought, expression, belief, faith and worship; EQUALITY of status and of opportunity; and to promote among them all FRATERNITY assuring the dignity of the individual and the unity and integrity of the Nation.

PART III — FUNDAMENTAL RIGHTS

Section 14: Equality before law.
The State shall not deny to any person equality before the law or the equal protection of the laws within the territory of India.

Section 19: Protection of certain rights regarding freedom of speech, etc.
(1) All citizens shall have the right—
(a) to freedom of speech and expression;
(b) to assemble peaceably and without arms;
(c) to form associations or unions or co-operative societies;
(d) to move freely throughout the territory of India;
(e) to reside and settle in any part of the territory of India.
(2) Nothing in sub-clause (a) of clause (1) shall affect the operation of any existing law, or prevent the State from making any law, in so far as such law imposes reasonable restrictions in the interests of the sovereignty and integrity of India, security of the State, or public order.

Section 21: Protection of life and personal liberty.
No person shall be deprived of his life or personal liberty except according to procedure established by law.
[Judicial Interpretation: Includes right to privacy (Puttaswamy 2017), right to clean environment, and procedural due process (Maneka Gandhi 1978)].

Section 32: Remedies for enforcement of rights conferred by this Part.
(1) The right to move the Supreme Court by appropriate proceedings for the enforcement of the rights conferred by this Part is guaranteed.
(2) The Supreme Court shall have power to issue directions or orders or writs, including writs in the nature of habeas corpus, mandamus, prohibition, quo warranto and certiorari, whichever may be appropriate, for the enforcement of any of the rights conferred by this Part.

PART VI — THE HIGH COURTS IN THE STATES

Section 226: Power of High Courts to issue certain writs.
(1) Notwithstanding anything in Article 32, every High Court shall have power, throughout the territories in relation to which it exercises jurisdiction, to issue to any person or authority, including in appropriate cases, any Government, within those territories directions, orders or writs, including writs in the nature of habeas corpus, mandamus, prohibition, quo warranto and certiorari, or any of them, for the enforcement of any of the rights conferred by Part III and for any other purpose.
